hi! welcome to my homepage under construction since 2011, and getting no closer

i'm anıl. i build frontends for a living, which mostly means i build the part of a product that nobody photographs: the dashboards, the forms, the component library three other teams quietly build on.

i started in istanbul in 2011 on banking software. now i'm in berlin, doing B2B parking platforms at APCOA Group, and in the evenings i work on a UI library of my own. same job the whole way through, really. take something complicated on the inside and make it look simple on the outside.

this page is plain html in a folder. tables, a frameset, a hit counter. i made it because it made me laugh, and because the proper site takes itself quite seriously. that one is at anilokay.com.
