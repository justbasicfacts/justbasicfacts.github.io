About me

who is behind all this

i grew up in istanbul and started working there in 2011, at VeriPark, on banking and CRM software. from there it was internet banking at ING Türkiye, then e-commerce at Ladenzeile in berlin, and since 2023 B2B parking at APCOA Group.

different domains every time, same job underneath: take something complicated on the inside and make it usable on the outside. i have been lucky enough to spend most of it on the part other developers later build on. a React component library at Ladenzeile, reusable web components in banking, and now a design system of my own in the evenings.

outside work it is bicycles, records, and an unreasonable interest in other people's design systems. that side of things lives on the off screen page.

*** how i work ***

  • One component everyone uses beats five that nearly match.
  • Write the decision down while it is still fresh, not six months later.
  • Treat the library as a product. It has users, and they file bugs.
  • Small migrations in reliable steps beat a rewrite that lands in a quarter.
