who am i?

I'm Anıl, a frontend developer in Berlin. Sixteen years in, and most of my work has been on the plumbing side of the frontend: dashboards people use every day, component libraries other teams build on, and a few migrations nobody wanted to start.

These days I'm at APCOA Group, working on B2B parking platforms. Before that: Istanbul, then Berlin. VeriPark, ING Türkiye, Ladenzeile. Different domains each time, same job: take something complicated on the inside and make it usable on the outside.

Outside of work I keep coming back to framework-agnostic UI: web components, design tokens, and the tooling around them.
